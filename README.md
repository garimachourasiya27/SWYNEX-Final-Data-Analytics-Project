<h2>1. Project Overview</h2>

<p>This project focuses on analyzing cafe sales data to understand revenue trends, product performance, customer payment preferences, and location-wise sales patterns.</p>

<p>Microsoft Excel was used for data cleaning and preparation, while Microsoft Power BI was used to create an interactive dashboard for visualizing key sales insights.</p>

<h2>2. Problem Statement</h2>

<p>Raw cafe sales data can contain missing values, inconsistent records, and information that is difficult to interpret without analysis.</p>

<p>This project aims to organize and analyze sales data to identify revenue trends, popular products, payment preferences, and opportunities to improve business performance.</p>

<h2>3. Project Objectives</h2>

<ul>
<li>Clean and prepare raw cafe sales data.</li>
<li>Analyze sales and revenue performance.</li>
<li>Identify popular products.</li>
<li>Understand monthly revenue trends.</li>
<li>Analyze customer payment preferences.</li>
<li>Compare sales across locations.</li>
<li>Create an interactive Power BI dashboard.</li>
<li>Generate business recommendations based on the analysis.</li>
</ul>

<h2>8. Data Analysis</h2>

<h3>8.3 Monthly Revenue Analysis</h3>
<p>Analyzed monthly revenue from January to December 2023 to identify high-performing and low-performing months.</p>

<h3>8.4 Payment Method Analysis</h3>
<p>Compared the payment methods recorded in the dataset to understand customer payment preferences.</p>

<h3>8.5 Location-Wise Analysis</h3>
<p>Compared sales across the available location categories to evaluate performance.</p>

<h2>12. Business Recommendations</h2>

<ul>
<li><strong>Inventory Management:</strong> Monitor stock levels for popular products, especially Juice.</li>
<li><strong>Monthly Sales Planning:</strong> Use monthly revenue trends to plan inventory and promotions.</li>
<li><strong>Digital Payments:</strong> Continue supporting digital payment options based on observed customer preferences.</li>
<li><strong>Product Promotions:</strong> Consider targeted promotions for products with comparatively lower revenue.</li>
<li><strong>Location Performance:</strong> Review location-wise performance to identify improvement opportunities.</li>
<li><strong>KPI Monitoring:</strong> Track revenue, transactions, and quantity sold regularly.</li>
<li><strong>Data-Driven Decisions:</strong> Use dashboard insights to support sales and inventory decisions.</li>
</ul>

<p>This project demonstrates how Microsoft Excel and Power BI can be used together for practical sales data analytics.</p>

<p>The project provides a structured view of cafe sales data and demonstrates how data analytics can support business monitoring and informed decision-making.</p>
